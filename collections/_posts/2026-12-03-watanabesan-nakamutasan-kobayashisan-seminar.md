## Physics-Inspired Approaches to Learning Dynamics: Toward an Understanding of Deep Learning {#english}
The NINJAL Mathematical Sciences Seminar #4, entitled “Physics-Inspired Approaches to Learning Dynamics: Toward an Understanding of Deep Learning,” will be held at the National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics (NINJAL) in Tachikawa on Thursday, December 3, 2026. We hope to provide a relaxed and informal setting for discussion. Anyone interested in the topic is welcome to attend. Please register in advance using the form below.
- 📅 Thursday, December 3, 2026, 13:30–17:30
- 📍 National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics (NINJAL), Tachikawa <https://www.ninjal.ac.jp/utility/access/>
- 🗣️ Taishi Watanabe (Kyoto U.), Asahi Nakamuta (Kyoto U.), and Shuta Kobayashi (Kyoto U.)
- 🌐 **Language**: All talks will be given in Japanese.
- 📝 **Program**:
  - 13:30–14:30 Taishi Watanabe
    “Training-Time Scaling Laws for Deep Linear Networks on Power-Law Data” (tentative)
    - Abstract: TBA
  - 14:30–15:00 Break
  - 15:00–16:00 Asahi Nakamuta
    “Learning reshapes power-law anisotropy in internal representations” (tentative)
    - Abstract: TBA
  - 16:00–16:30 Break
  - 16:30–17:30 Shuta Kobayashi
    “Fisher-Information Flow Speed Limits in Stochastic Gradient Descent” (tentative)
    - Abstract: TBA
- **Organizers**: Kai Nakaishi (RIKEN), Ryo Karakida (AIST), Hitomi Kinoshita (NINJAL), and Sho Yokoi (NINJAL)
